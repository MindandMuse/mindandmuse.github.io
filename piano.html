CHOOSE YOUR APPROACH

Two ways to learn.
One personalised experience.

01

Classical Approach

A structured approach for students who want to build a strong, well-rounded foundation in piano.

Lessons develop technique, posture, hand position, music reading, rhythm, musical understanding and classical repertoire — building your skills progressively over time.

02

Learn Your Favourite Pieces

A flexible approach for students who mainly want to play the music they love.

Choose the pieces you'd like to learn, and we'll work on the techniques and skills that help you learn them successfully — while developing your playing along the way.

Not sure which approach is right for you? We can discuss your goals and find the best fit during your trial lesson.

THE MIND & MUSE APPROACH

Learn with purpose.
Play with expression.

With more than 10 years of piano teaching experience, including teaching at a conservatory in Esch since 2019, lessons are tailored to each student's goals, experience and pace of learning.

I believe that learning the piano should be challenging without becoming overwhelming. Mistakes are a natural part of the process — they help us understand, adjust and improve.

Whether you're learning your first piece, returning to the piano after a break, or preparing for a performance, the aim is to help you develop strong skills while becoming a confident and expressive musician.
