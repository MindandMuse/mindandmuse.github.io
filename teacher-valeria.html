ABOUT VALERIA

Teaching with
purpose and empathy.

With over 10 years of teaching experience and a Bachelor of Music with a focus on music pedagogy and piano teaching, Valeria has taught students of all ages and levels — from beginners discovering the piano for the first time to advanced students developing confident and expressive performance.

Her approach is personal and encouraging: understanding each student's goals, strengths and challenges, and finding the right way to help them progress. She believes mistakes are a natural and valuable part of learning — not something to be afraid of, but an opportunity to understand, improve and grow.

Alongside piano, Valeria teaches English from Italian, French, German and Spanish. She speaks six languages fluently and is currently learning a seventh, giving her first-hand insight into the hurdles that come with learning a new language — from pronunciation and grammar to building the confidence to actually speak.

Lessons are practical, supportive and adapted to each student's needs, whether the goal is everyday conversation, academic study, professional communication, musical development or performance.
