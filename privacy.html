Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Mind & Muse respects your privacy and aims to handle your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you use the Mind & Muse website or services, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Mind & Muse, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Booking and lesson information
  • Your learning goals, interests and preferences
  • Lesson progress, teacher feedback and achievements
  • Information you provide when contacting us
  • Payment and transaction information associated with your bookings

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to provide and manage our services.

How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Arrange and manage lessons
  • Process bookings and payments
  • Provide access to your student account
  • Prepare lessons around your needs and goals
  • Provide lesson materials and track your progress
  • Communicate with you about lessons and bookings
  • Maintain and improve our services

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Mind & Muse uses third-party services to provide certain parts of our website and services. These may include:

  • Cal.com — lesson bookings and scheduling
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Supabase — student accounts and related data
  • Muzie — online piano lessons
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Google Forms — English placement testing

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Some providers may process information outside Australia.

Payments

Payments are processed through our payment provider. Mind & Muse does not directly store your full payment card details.

Student information

Information relating to lessons, progress, feedback and learning materials is used to provide personalised teaching.

Access to this information is limited to the student and authorised Mind & Muse teachers or administrators who need it to provide the service.

Please do not provide sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your lesson or service.

Website and cookies

Our website itself is primarily a static website. However, third-party services used by Mind & Muse may use cookies or similar technologies for functions such as authentication, booking, payment processing and website operation.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Access, correction and deletion

You can contact Mind & Muse if you would like to ask about personal information we hold about you or request that information be corrected or deleted, where appropriate.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Overseas processing

Some third-party service providers used by Mind & Muse may store or process information outside Australia. Where applicable, their own privacy policies explain how they handle personal information and where it may be processed.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact:

mindandmuse.info@gmail.com

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page.
