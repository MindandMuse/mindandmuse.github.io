ABOUT RICHARD

Understand the game,
not just the moves.

Richard has been passionate about chess since childhood. Largely self-taught in his early years, he has since competed in tournaments and developed a strong understanding of the game through years of playing and practice.

His teaching is practical, supportive and adapted to each student's level. Rather than simply memorising moves, students learn how to think in chess, make decisions and understand the ideas behind their moves.

A typical lesson can include reviewing a game, learning one key concept such as tactics or openings, and then putting it into practice through play. This keeps lessons clear, relaxed and focused on real improvement without overwhelming students with theory.

Richard believes chess can be more than a game: it can encourage focus, patience, problem-solving and the ability to stay calm when making decisions.
