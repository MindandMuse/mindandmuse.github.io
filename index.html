MY TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Learn in a way that
works for you.

Every learner is different. I first want to understand your goals, your difficulties and what you want to achieve. From there, we work out together an approach that makes sense for you.

I believe in useful practice — not simply doing exercises for the sake of doing them. What we learn should have a clear purpose and be something you can actually use.

With regular practice and the right guidance, you can build real skills and, just as importantly, confidence in your own ability to learn and improve.

“The goal isn't simply to learn something new — it's to feel confident using it.”
